## Answer capsule

> **Quick Answer:** Clogs usually come from cold oil, overheating, or a blocked airway — not “mystery hardware failure.” Warm the cart gently, clear the mouthpiece carefully, take slower pulls, and reseat on a charged battery. If it still fails after a licensed purchase, validate authenticity and contact support rather than buying unverified replacements.
